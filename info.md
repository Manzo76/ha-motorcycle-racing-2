# Motorcycle Racing

Follow MotoGP, Moto2, Moto3, MotoE, WorldSBK, British Superbikes or any other
motorcycle championship from your dashboard.

- Next round, with every session of the weekend and its start time
- Last race classification and podium
- Rider and team championship standings
- A calendar entity covering the whole season
- Race weekend, race day and session-live binary sensors
- A custom Lovelace card that installs itself — start-light countdown, timing
  board typography, championship bars

Add the integration once per series. Settings → Devices & services → Add
integration → **Motorcycle Racing**.
